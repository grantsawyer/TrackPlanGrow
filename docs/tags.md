# Tags

The Tags page (Planning section, after Budgets): tags on transactions, their cards and budgets, the calendar (shared with
the Overview's calendar card), colors and calendar labels. Tags as lines on Budgets are in `docs/planning.md`.

Moved from `docs/transactions.md` on 2026-11-05 (entries unchanged). Dated notes say when and why something was decided,
usually at the user's request; they stay so a choice isn't reversed by accident. When you change this area, update
this file in the same change.

- **Tags (migration 0048, user's request 2026-10-28; `tags.py`, `web/tags.py`):** your own labels on transactions,
  emoji welcome (`tags`: name unique ignoring case, 1-40 characters, spaces collapsed; `transaction_tags`: any number per
  transaction; deleting a transaction or a tag drops its links; both tables have the app_revision triggers; investment
  activity's links are in `investment_transaction_tags` (migration 0052, 2026-10-31), read with them as negative ids via
  `tags.LINKS_SQL`, so its rows tag, filter and sum on the Tags page like the rest). Tags only
  group: they never change a category or kind. **Transactions page:** a row shows its tags as chips after the
  description (`_tags.html` `tag_chips`, `#txn-tags-N`); the edit panel's Tags line (`_txn_tag_line.html`) lists them
  with a × each and a box (datalist of your tags; a new name creates the tag) with Add: `POST
  /transactions/{id}/tags` and `/tags/remove` return the line plus the row's chips out of band (hx-swap-oob), so the
  panel stays open; Enter in the box saves the panel and adds the tag (`save_edit` `new_tag`). Filter `tag` (after
  Category, only when tags exist; carried by group edit): **a checklist like the accounts'** (user's request 2026-10-30;
  was a select), any number of tags plus **"No tag"** (`none`: transactions without one), none ticked = all;
  `_tag_filter` / `_tag_where` (comma-joined ids and `none`, OR'ed). Group edit: a tag box
  + "Add tag" / "Remove tag" (`POST /transactions/bulk-tag`, checked rows or every match). Tags follow a pending
  transaction to its posted one (`sync._upsert_transaction`) and a removed one to its return (`removals.use_return`).
  **Rewards on Credit cards (user's requests 2026-11-03 / 04; `rewards.redeemed_summaries`):** a card that used rewards or
  got cash rewards in the range gets a row under its head: **Redeemed** = the value of its rewards-used transactions
  (rebates and credits), with its share of the cost in parentheses (value / (Spent + value): Spent is net of them) and
  "N¢ each"; **Cash rewards** = money in filed as income on the card that rewards didn't pay (a cash sign-up bonus). Those
  pairs are ordinary spending in the table (user's request 2026-11-04): cost and rebate sit in their category, each at its
  rate, netting to zero; no label or special group. Points turned into cash are a credit filed as Credit Card Rewards
  income, which earns nothing anyway; Rewards used on it only records the points (value per point, Redeemed, balance). Bonus points join
  the Bonuses group (linked to the card's page). Tags count a rewards-paid purchase like any other (its cost is tagged).
  **Tags page (Planning, after Budgets since 2026-11-02; `/tags`):** the calendar sits 16px above the cards, like the cards' own gap, at any scroll position of their list (its grid has no margins of its own there; 2026-11-04). A search box in the ribbon, left of the calendar's view toggle (user's request 2026-11-04; ui.js `[data-tag-search]`), hides the cards whose name, Start or End date (as shown, "Sep 12", and as month and year, "September 2026") doesn't contain what you type (any case; `data-tag-text`; "No tags match." when none do), kept for the tab across reloads (sessionStorage). A card per tag, newest first (its own End date, else its latest tagged date; user's request 2026-10-28; `tags.summaries`): the tag
  (link to its transactions, `range=custom` from its first to last date; a tag whose Start date is still to come adds
  how far off it is after its name, muted (`web/tags.countdown`, user's requests 2026-11-04): "(21d away)" up to 28 days,
  then weeks to 3 months "(7.5w away)", then months "(3.5m away)", weeks and months to the nearest half), "N transactions · first – last", Spent (Σ
  expense, refunds lower it, + transfers out) and Received (−Σ income + transfers in, only when there is any) as the
  Credit cards measures. **Transfers count (user's request 2026-10-28)**, by direction (Plaid's sign: out = spent, in =
  received; card payments are transfers too). Spent by category then a "Received" group (`TagLine.side`), each row a
  link to exactly those transactions (`only=1`, the transactions' kind) with a one-colour share bar and %; only loan
  entries and transactions waiting for review (kind `excluded`) are left out ("N not counted"); hidden accounts are
  left out. Rename (pencil) and delete (trash can; the transactions just lose the tag) at the card's foot. Each card is in its tag's colour (the section
  carries `tb-N` from `tag_slots`, the calendar's colour): a swatch before its name and its share bars
  (user's request 2026-10-28).
  **Calendar card** above the tag cards (user's requests 2026-10-28; `web/tags.calendar_month`): a month grid, Sunday
  first. **A tag's own Start and End dates** (migration 0050, user's request 2026-10-28; date fields on their own row under

  fires "change" mid-typing, a one-digit year being a valid date; years outside 1900-2200 are refused), `POST /tags/{id}/dates` back to the same month; `tags.set_dates`: either may be
  blank, start <= end) make it one bar from Start to End (spent = its days inside), and each day with its transactions
  outside them a single-day bar (`Run.loose`: lighter, dashed edge, "(outside its dates)" on hover); a tag with no or
  one date: a bar per **run of consecutive days** with its transactions (`tags.calendar_items`; a day on its own is a
  one-day bar; visible accounts, any kind). Totals on the cards always count every tagged transaction. Like multi-day events, packed into
  lanes week by week (earlier starts first, then longer), linking to that run's transactions (from / to = its days),
  hover = its dates and everything spent on the tag, the card's Spent (user's request 2026-11-04: a trip's bar showed $0
  when the spending, such as flights booked earlier, fell outside its dates).
  A bar continuing from / into another week has a square end. One colour per tag, by creation order (`tag_slots`:
  series 1-8, gray after; `.tb-N` tint behind the name, text stays in the text colour). **Views** (ribbon toggle, user's request 2026-10-28; `CALENDAR_VIEWS`, `?view=month|quarter|year&month=` the anchor):
  1 month = one grid with named bars; 3 months = the three months ending with the anchor, side by side (stacked under 900px
  of card), bars named in smaller type; 1 year = the anchor's calendar year as twelve small grids (4 / 3 / 2 across by
  card width), bars as thin unnamed strips (hover and link still work). No legend (removed, user's request 2026-10-29:
  colour and hover tell the tags apart). Arrows step 1 month in the 1-month and 3-month views (the 3-month view since 2026-11-02) and 12 in the 1-year view, opening on the
  month of the latest tagged date; a tag's date form returns to the same view and anchor. Built server-side with column classes
  (`.cs-N` / `.ce-N`), no inline styles; the day lines are the week box's repeating background. All time (no
  range control: tags usually mark one trip or project).
  **Calendar settings** (user's request 2026-10-29): a gear right of the arrows opens a popup (ui.js `data-popover`) with
  "Show transaction dates", saved in `app_settings` 'tags_calendar_days' (`tags.calendar_shows_days`; on by default;

  **New tag** (user's request 2026-10-29): + right of the ribbon toggle (always there, even with no tags) drops down a

  no transactions until you tag some on Transactions; it can get dates on its card first.
  **The cards (user's request 2026-11-02; replaced Upcoming / Visible / Past; `web/tags.card_order`):** one list, newest first by End date, else latest tagged date, else Start; a tag with none comes first. It has its own scrolling area, so the calendar stays in view. Tags drawn on the calendar's current view are outlined in their color, and `placeTagCards` (ui.js) scrolls the list so the first is at its top, adding room below if needed. It follows the arrows, the toggle and the setting. After a save on a card the list keeps its place (sessionStorage).
- **Tag budgets (Tags cards; migration 0057, user's requests 2026-11-02):** a card has a total (the whole tag; blank with category budgets = their sum) and category budgets (`tag_category_budgets`; a category's includes subcategories without their own; blank or its trash can, which asks first, removes it and what was allocated to it). **View and edit modes (user's request 2026-11-04):** the budgets live in the card's table (Category | Budget | Amount | Share): a **Total** row first, then each category budget, then Other (the total, "N% used", ✕ when over). The pencil, after the name and countdown (2026-11-04; the heading a centred flex row, so it sits at the name's middle, 2px away; it and Done are `a[data-keep-place]`, so the page and the card list keep their place, as after a save: keep-scroll.js, ui.js), opens edit mode (`?edit=<id>`, which each save keeps: `_back(edit=)`): the name, Start / End and every budget become boxes saved on leaving them (each its own hidden form, by `form=`, as a form can't wrap a row), a last row adds a category budget, and the tag's trash can and Done appear; view mode reads the dates ("Oct 17 – Nov 1", `.tag-when`; each table row shows its own count, the category budgets', Other's and Total's included, 2026-11-04; right under the name in the head's left column, packed to its top, 2px apart; the name 1.3rem and this line 1.15rem, italic, 16px above the table; the first – last tagged dates under the name went, 2026-11-04) and has only the pencil. The Total row is the table's first; one 10px gap throughout: headings → Total, Total → first category, last category → Other (and Received); edit mode's name box fills the heading beside the swatch (`.tag-title-edit`); no count there, and each box is pulled out by its border + padding so its text lines up with the table's (dates and the add row's select on the left, budgets on the right, under the Budget heading). Total, Other and Received
  rows are the categories' font size (their headings would be 0.85rem). The table's only line is under its column headings. The bars compare with the card's Spent, which includes transfers out (the Budgets lines count expense-kind only). **Category budgets are part of the total (user's request 2026-11-04):** the rest of it (total − category budgets, `TagSummary.other_budget_cents`) covers every other category. A category budget that would take them past the total comes back to the card to ask (`tags.BudgetTooSmall`, `raise_*` query, `.tag-raise`): Raise budget and save (`raise=1`) sets the total to their sum, Cancel leaves both; a total below their sum is refused. The breakdown then has a Budget column, before Amount: each category budget's row (its budget, ✕ before it when over so the numbers line up, then what it spent, its subcategories included), then an **Other** group (what they spent against the rest; its figures, headings, take the compact cells' 8px right
  padding so they line up with the rows', as on Credit cards) listing each category used (`TagLine.bucket` None), then Received. Months get their amounts on the Budgets page: `docs/planning.md`.
- **Tag colors (migration 0058, user's request 2026-11-02):** the swatch on a tag's card opens a picker: 36 predefined colors (`tags.COLOR_PALETTE`, 12 hues in 3 shades), your colors (`tag_custom_colors`, shared by every tag; × removes one from the list and tags using it keep it), Automatic (`tag_slots`, as before) and a color box whose Add saves the color to your list and gives it to the tag (`POST /tags/{id}/color`). `tags.color` holds '#rrggbb'; `data-tag-color` on the card and calendar bars sets `--tb` from ui.js (the CSP allows no inline styles). One value in both themes, so unlike series colors these have no separate light and dark steps: the lightest shades are faint on the light theme and the darkest on the dark theme.
- **Inactive tags (user's request 2026-11-02; `tags.split_by_activity`):** a tag whose latest transaction in a visible account (investment activity included) is more than 365 days old. The Transactions Tags filter lists them last under an "Inactive" header. The edit panel's and group edit's suggestions, the rule form's and Budgets' + Add budget leave them out (the first three are `<datalist>`s, which can't show a header, so this holds on the Transactions page too); typing an old tag's full name still uses it. A tag with no transactions is active. The Tags page and a tag already on the budget aren't affected.
- **Calendar labels (migration 0059, user's requests 2026-11-02; `calendar_labels.py`):** each day of the Tags calendar shows, on the date's row, your labels, then US federal holidays, then major shopping days (italic), with a grey background unless the day has only shopping days, worked out by rule for any year with no service. Fixed-date federal holidays on a weekend also show an estimated "(observed)" day by the federal rule (Saturday -> Friday before, Sunday -> Monday after, so New Year's Day can mark the previous December 31); employers and banks can differ. The 1-year view's small grids underline the day's number, names on hover. Clicking a day opens `#day-dialog`: add a label, one day or Every year (from its date on; February 29 only in leap years), or remove one of yours; holidays can't be removed. Prime Day and similar aren't included, as a retailer sets their dates.
- **Today on the calendars (user's request 2026-11-02):** a blue background (`--accent` at 18%) and a blue outline (user's requests 2026-11-02, Overview first, then the Tags page; the net worth chart's area is 10%, which looked fainter on a small box) on today's whole box, on the Tags page and Overview's card: `.cal-today-layer` spans the week's row, above the day and below the bars, and takes no clicks.
- **Overview's calendar card (user's request 2026-11-02; `web/tags.month_card`, `_overview_calendar.html`, shared macros `_tag_calendar.html`):** under Accounts: one month, this month by default, with the Tags calendar's bars and labels (compact grid). The arrows swap only the card (`GET /overview/calendar`; `?cal=YYYY-MM` for a full page), and a Today link shows on other months. Clicking a day adds or removes a label and returns here in the same month. Below it, or to its right when the card is at least 640px wide (container query; the header then ends where the calendar does, so the arrows stay over it): two countdowns, like "Trip in" over "23 days" (the name over "Today" on the day). Below the calendar each is centered in its half of the card, side by side at every width. Beside the calendar their text is larger, 56px apart, and centered in the column across and on the card's full height. By default they show the next two things coming up (`web/tags.countdowns`): tags by their Start date from today on (never one without dates), and holidays and your labels at their next date, shopping days included. A timer's title opens a list by Events, Holidays and Your dates, with Next up for automatic; choosing posts `POST /overview/countdown` (app_settings `overview_countdown_<slot>`, the date it was on and its key) and swaps the card. A timer on automatic skips what the other shows; what the other is set to is grayed out. Every choice goes back to automatic once its date passes (user's rule 2026-11-02), holidays and yearly labels too; a tag moved to a later date stays chosen.
- **Rows line up, each day once (user's request 2026-11-02):** in the 3-month and 1-year views each month's title, weekday letters and weeks share the row's tracks (CSS subgrid), so a week is the same height across the row. Another month's grayed day is blank where its own month shows it (`calendar_month` `hide`), e.g. Mar 1-6, 2027 at the end of February; bars and today are cut there too, and its grid lines are covered (`.cal-blank-layer`, the card's background; the line above it stays, as the shown week's edge). It stays where its own month isn't shown: the range's edges, or a dropped row's day (Aug 1, 2026 at the end of July).
- **Clicking a bar (user's request 2026-11-02; replaced opening its transactions):** on the Tags page it scrolls the cards so its tag's card is in the top row (ui.js; `#tag-card-<id>` without scripts); on Overview's calendar it opens the Tags page on that month with `?focus=<id>`, which puts that card on top. The tag's name on its card still opens its transactions.
- **Today on the Tags calendar (user's request 2026-11-02):** a Today link between the arrows, shown when you've moved away: this month (1 month), this month and the next 2 (3 months), this year (1 year).
- **Rows per month (user's request 2026-11-02; `web/tags.month_rows`):** the months shown have the same number of rows, on the Tags page and Overview. A month needing 6 drops the end row with fewer of its own days (a tie drops the last), so August 2026 loses the row with only Aug 1 (it shows as a grayed day at the end of July). A row with the range's first or last day, or today, is kept, so a month whose two ends both hold one keeps 6, as in the 1-month view. The exception is February, which can need only 4 rows and keeps 4 (user's rule 2026-11-02; it was briefly filled out with March's days).
